Direction C — Warm Indian

Ivory background. Deep royal indigo primary, saffron accents, warm neutrals. Serif accents on hero words for warmth. Feels like CRED or a grown-up lifestyle brand — emotive without being cheesy, distinctly Indian without being flag-waving. Best for mass-market consumers, retail investors, lifestyle brand partners.
